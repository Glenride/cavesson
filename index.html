Cavesson Vault is a wallet for founders, small operators, and their AI agents whose rules are 7 layers of protection. When your agents spend, hire, and sign, the Vault checks every action against rules you set first — and leaves a receipt for what happened.

Machines propose, but humans are the source of all decisions.

How the Vault works

  1. 1

    House rules, set by the Human first

    You set the budget and the allowlist before your agents run.

  2. 2

    A gate, checked before execution

    Anything outside the rules is stopped, not warned about afterward.

  3. 3

    Attested receipts, for allowed and stopped actions

    Every action leaves a receipt naming what was proposed and who decided.
