The security story

A cavesson is the noseband a trainer puts on a powerful animal to keep it under a human's hand while it's being trained. It is the piece that says: this strength answers to a person.

Why a noseband

Name and story locked 26 October 2026.

Your data is your life. Your family. Your money. Your work. Your future. You wouldn't hand your house keys to a stranger, or let someone you don't know walk through your home and read your papers. So why is AI any different?

That's what happens when your agents live in someone else's cloud. Your data sits on machines you don't own, behind doors you don't lock. Hope is not a lock. Cavesson is the lock: your agents live in your home, behind your lock, under your hand. Every job is checked against what you asked for before anything goes out. Every job comes back with proof. Nothing leaves without your say.

Machines propose, but humans are the source of all decisions.

The next chapter is proof that doesn't require handing your data to anyone: the record of what an agent did, checked without seeing your data at all. Proof that protects you instead of exposing you.

What Cavesson is: a lock. A crew. A checkpoint. The thing that keeps a powerful team under a human's hand.

What Cavesson isn't: not a cloud. Not an open door. Not a black box. Not a replacement for the human.
